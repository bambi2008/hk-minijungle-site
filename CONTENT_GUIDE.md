# Content guide

## Add or update a product

Edit the `products` array in `src/content.js`. Keep one stable lowercase slug, provide independent `en` and `zh-hk` content, and choose only a truthful status: `pilot`, `development`, `validation` or `prototype`. The image `evidence` must match the actual asset.

## Add or update a solution

Edit the `solutions` array. Describe the real setting problem, suitable and unsuitable conditions, related product slugs, and one conversion label per language. Concept scenes must keep the visible concept label.

## Add a proof note

Edit `proofNotes`. A useful note needs: what was tested, why it matters, method, observation and limitations. Do not publish a result until the real method, date and product version are supplied.

## Add a journal post

Edit `journalPosts`. New drafts must remain marked `draft` until title, body, author, dates, cover-image rights and SEO copy are approved. Avoid manufacturing company news when the source is an idea or development note.

## Replace an image

1. Add the optimized file under `public/images/`.
2. Record owner, copyright, capture/render date, product/version and public-use approval.
3. Update the content item path and evidence type.
4. Check the image at desktop and mobile crops.
5. Remove the concept label only when the replacement is approved real photography.

## Translation

Write English and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese naturally. Do not mirror sentence structure mechanically. Keep product names in English and use Hong Kong terms such as `空間`, `養護`, `查詢`, `私隱` and `辦公室`.
